# **VortyxHorizon – vCIO & Strategic Roadmap**

**Division:** VortyxMSP (Services)  
**Architecture:** Strategic Planning & Visualization  
**Status:** [PLANNING]

## **1. Executive Summary**

VortyxHorizon acts as a "vCIO" (Virtual Chief Information Officer). It shifts the conversation from "Maintenance" to "Strategy". It visualizes IT budgets, forecasts technical debt, and manages the long-term Strategic Roadmap for the client.

## **2. Core Features**

### **2.1 Holistic Health Scoring**

- **Health Score:** A weighted algorithm (Performance, Security, Lifecycle) that provides a single 0-100 score for an organization's technology stack.
- **Drill-Down:** Visual navigation from a high-level score down to specific failing Configuration Items (CIs).

### **2.2 Budget Forecasting**

- **The Budget Wall:** A 3-year visual roadmap of anticipated hardware and software refresh costs.
- **Lifecycle Management:** Automatically identifies aging assets (from VortyxPulse) and calculates replacement costs.

### **2.3 Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)**

- **Automated Reports:** Pixel-perfect PDF reports for client presentations.
- **Strategy Mapping:** Links technical initiatives to business goals.

## **3. Integration with Vortyx Ecosystem**

- **VortyxPulse:** Provides the raw asset lifecycle data (purchase dates, warranty status) for budget forecasting.
- **VortyxPilot:** Strategic projects identified in Horizon can be converted into actionable tickets and projects in Pilot.
- **VortyxNexus:** References documented infrastructure to build accurate technology roadmaps.
- **VortyxShield:** Incorporates compliance gaps into the strategic roadmap.
